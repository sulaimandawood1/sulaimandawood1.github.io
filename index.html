About

I work where construction meets controls. At American Contracting and Environmental Services (ACE), I lead construction administration and startup on a municipal wastewater treatment plant upgrade: RFIs, submittals, startup plans, I/O and loop testing, change orders, and the schedule that ties them together.

Before construction, I was a researcher. At Clarkson University, on a NASA-funded program, I studied how concrete made from lunar regolith simulants behaves at the micro scale. That work taught me something I use on every jobsite: performance is decided in the details.

I grew up in Azad Kashmir, studied civil engineering at UET Taxila, and started my career on roadway and building sites in Pakistan before moving to the United States for graduate school.

Research

Geopolymer concrete from lunar regolith simulants

Developed alkali-activated binders from simulated lunar soil, reaching compressive strengths near 40 MPa, and tested how mixture design and curing change strength with additive manufacturing in mind.

  • Nanoindentation
  • SEM-EDS
  • XRD
  • Mix design

M.S. thesis, ASCE Journal of Aerospace Engineering paper, Earth & Space 2024

Concrete returned from low Earth orbit

Contributed to the analysis of cementitious and regolith-based samples exposed outside the International Space Station as part of MISSE-15.

  • Micromechanics
  • Microstructure
  • Space exposure

Earth & Space 2026 paper, accepted

Belitic calcium sulfoaluminate cement
